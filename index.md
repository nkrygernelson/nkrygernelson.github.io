# Hi, I'm Nicholas 👋

I study mathematical modelling at DTU in Denmark. At the moment I'm most
interested in diffusion models — the mathematics behind them and what happens
when you actually implement the pieces yourself.

## Featured: a crash course in diffusion

I'm writing a series on the mathematics of modern diffusion models — starting
from the probabilistic problem they solve and building up to SDEs, score
matching, the Fokker–Planck equation and flow matching.
