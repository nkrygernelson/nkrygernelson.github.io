# welcome to my blog: GuinHole

I study mathematical modelling at DTU in Denmark. At the moment I'm most
interested in diffusion models. I will try to post some tutorials and
implementations of recent papers.

## Featured: a crash course in diffusion

I'm writing a series on the mathematics of modern diffusion models, starting
from the probabilistic problem they solve and building up to SDEs, score
matching, the Fokker-Planck equation and flow matching.

- **[Part 1: DDPM and fundamentals](./diffpart1.html)**
- **[Part 2: Score and SDEs](./diffpart2.html)**
- **[Part 3: Flow Matching](./diffpart3.html)**
- **[Part 4: Unification](./diffpart4.html)** (work in progress)
- **[Part 5: Solvers](./diffpart5.html)** (work in progress)

Start at [the series index](./diffusion.html).
